About Me

I am a Strategic Communications and Digital Marketing professional with over three years of experience focused on achieving measurable impact within the NGO and development sector. My unique value lies in bridging the gap between high-level policy objectives and technical digital execution. I ensure every communication strategy is not only cohesive but also optimized for online visibility and audience engagement.

My expertise is a powerful combination of content strategy and technical mastery. I spearhead end-to-end multimedia production — including video, graphic design, and photography — and am proficient in WordPress and SEO best practices, consistently maximizing organizational reach and online discovery. My drive is to convert challenging organizational narratives into engaging, accessible, and optimized digital realities that contribute to sustainable success. I am actively seeking a role where my blended expertise in strategic NGO communications, digital marketing, and technical content production can directly fuel institutional growth.
