About Me

Programme Delivery. Communications Impact

I'm a programmes and communications professional working where delivery, storytelling, and technology meet in the development sector. At the Drylands Learning and Capacity Building Initiative, I've led core activities across multi-organisation programmes — including convening the Youth Community of Practice — while driving the communications that carry our work to donors and policymakers.

I bring an unusually complete toolkit to programme work. Alongside coordinating activities and stakeholder engagement, I've designed a monitoring and evaluation system, grown our digital reach by over 600%, led communications for the Pastoralist Leadership Summit (100,000+ interactions), produced 20 institutional reports, built four websites, and administer the full Microsoft 365 environment for our 20-person organisation. It means I can deliver a programme, communicate its impact, and build the systems that run it — without needing three separate hires.

I'm deepening this foundation with a Master's in Project Planning and Management, building on my experience to move further into programme leadership. Fluent in English and Swahili, and a member of the Public Relations Society of Kenya.
