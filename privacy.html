Last updated: 28 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hillary Kitel ("I", "me", "my") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you visit kitel.site or contact me through this website.

Who I Am

This is the personal portfolio website of Hillary Pchumba Kitel, based in Nairobi, Kenya. You can reach me at pchumbakitel@gmail.com regarding any privacy matter or this policy.

What Data I Collect

I only collect personal data that you choose to provide through the contact form on this site. This includes:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The subject and content of your message

I do not use tracking cookies, analytics, or advertising on this site, and I do not collect data automatically in the background.

How Your Data Is Handled

The contact form on this site opens your own email application with your message pre-filled — it does not store your information on any server or database owned by this site. When you send the message, it is delivered to my personal email inbox (pchumbakitel@gmail.com) through your chosen email provider.

How I Use Your Data

I use the information you send solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or message
  • Follow up on opportunities, collaborations, or requests (such as a CV request)

I never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

How Long I Keep Your Data

I keep email correspondence only as long as necessary to respond to and follow up on your enquiry. You may ask me to delete your correspondence at any time.

Data Security

Your messages are stored within my personal email account, which is protected by strong passwords and standard security measures provided by the email provider.

Third-Party Services

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Media content may link to external platforms such as YouTube and Google Drive, which have their own privacy policies. This site embeds YouTube videos, which may set cookies through YouTube once you interact with them — please refer to Google's privacy policy for details.

Content & Attribution

The work, projects, reports, media, and case studies featured on this site were produced during my professional engagements and are shared with the awareness and approval of the organisations and teams I have worked with. All institutional work remains the intellectual property of the respective organisations. This portfolio presents selected work to demonstrate my professional contribution and capability.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of the personal data you have shared with me, and to object to its use. To exercise any of these rights, email me at pchumbakitel@gmail.com.

Changes To This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact me at pchumbakitel@gmail.com.
